Player Bio

Hi! I'm David, a second-year Computer Science student who fell in love with the web the day I discovered that a few lines of CSS can make a button feel good to press. Since then I've been building things for the browser and chasing that feeling.

I enjoy the space where design meets code: responsive layouts, accessible components, small animations and design systems that stay tidy as they grow. Last summer I worked as a front-end intern on a React dashboard, and my team took first place at a campus hackathon with a student carpool app.

Outside the editor you'll find me playing retro games (hence this card), sketching UI ideas in Figma, or teaching friends that display: grid is not scary.
